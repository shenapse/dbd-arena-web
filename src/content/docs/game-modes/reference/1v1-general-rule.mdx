The shared general rule for 1v1 game modes (Symmetric, Asymmetric, and future
1v1 formats). It applies to every killer across those modes. Per-mode and
per-killer detail (maps, win conditions, perk and add-on limits) lives in
each game mode's own balancing pages, which complement this rule.

## Killer ping

The killer must hold a stable connection below 60 ms for the trial to
count.
Persistent high ping or instability is handled as a remake.

## Starting a round

<Aside type="note" title="Summary">
  The round follows the common "crouch and response" starting convention at Killer Shack.
</Aside>

Each round must begin at Killer Shack. The survivor chooses one of the
shack's two doorways and takes position there. The killer must take
position at the opposite doorway.
The survivor has the right to choose which doorway they use before the
round begins.

Once both players are in position, the killer must nod to indicate they are ready before starting. 
The survivor then gives the start signal
by performing one quick crouch. Upon seeing the signal, the killer must
immediately perform the designated start response.

The designated start response—such as missing a basic attack or using
the killer's power—is specified in the applicable per-killer rule.

## Starting the chase timer

The chase timer starts when the survivor performs the start signal. If
the killer does not perform the designated response, or performs it
noticeably late, the players must reset and repeat the starting
procedure.

## Basic Rules

- No map resources may be used or broken before the start of a round (including generators, chests, etc.).
- You may not influence the map layout before the start of a round (for example, bamboozling a window).
- You may not close the hatch.

### Don't stay hidden rule

Survivors may not stay hidden to earn chase time:
- Survivors may not walk, crouch, or stand still for more than 15 seconds at a time while outside the killer's line of sight. After 15 seconds of walking, crouching, or standing still, they must run for at least 5 seconds.
- If a survivor has been in the killer's line of sight for more than 15 seconds and outside the killer's terror radius for more than 15 seconds, they must immediately make a loud-noise notification.
- These timers reset when the killer interacts with an object on the map (for example, by breaking a pallet).

## Rule Enforcement

If a rule violation occurs (except as listed below), the non-offending player may replay that round.

- Exception:
  - Don't stay hidden rule

If there is a dispute, video evidence must be presented.

### Chase time compensation

- All additional time earned by violating the "Don't stay hidden rule" is deducted from the survivor's chase time.
- The violating state is cleared when any of the following occurs:
  - the pursuit restarts
  - the killer enters the Undetectable state

In particular, stealth killers may claim a violation only after their stealth ability ends.

## Surrender

Surrender is made by disconnecting.

The survivor may surrender at any time.
The killer may surrender once achieving their applicable win condition
is no longer possible.

A surrender causes the surrendering player to lose the current round.
It does not forfeit the entire match.

- DBD 1v1 Asymmetric Ranked
