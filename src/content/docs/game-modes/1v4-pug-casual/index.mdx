## Game structure

| Aspect | Value |
|---|---|
| Team structure | 1 × Killer vs 4 × Survivors |
| Survivor team structure | 4 individually queued players. They do not need to belong to a party. |
| Map selection | Fixed per killer character |
| Voice comms | Yes — all four survivors are assumed to be in voice with one another. |
| Pre-match killer reveal | Yes — the killer character is known to both sides before the match starts. |
| Pre-match player reveal | Yes |

## Queue requirement

Both roles currently queue solo.

| Who | How they queue | Declares at queue time |
|---|---|---|
| Killer player | Queues solo. | One killer character. |
| Survivor player | Queues solo. | Nothing. |

A future update will allow survivor parties of two, three, or four players.
Any remaining survivor slots will be filled by solo-queued or party-queued players.

## Result facts

| Fact | Notes |
|---|---|
| Winning side | Which side won — killers or survivors. Omitted on a draw. |
| Draw | Marks a drawn match. Mutually exclusive with a winning side. |
| Generators remaining | How many generators were left unrepaired at the end (0-5). |
| Hook stages | Total hook stages performed by the killer during the match (0-12). |
| Kills | How many survivors were killed (0-4). |

## Ratings

1v4 PUG Casual uses ratings that are both game-mode specific and killer specific.

The same rating scope is used in both phases:

- **Matchmaking phase:** players are matched using their 1v4 PUG Casual rating for the relevant killer.
- **Rating update phase:** that same scoped rating is updated after the match.
