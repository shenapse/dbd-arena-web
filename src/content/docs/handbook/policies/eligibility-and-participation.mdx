The requirements for joining Arena and remaining eligible to play. These
requirements apply across all game modes unless a game mode states additional
requirements.

## Supported region

Arena currently supports players in **Asia**. Players must be able to take part
reliably in the region's matchmaking and scheduled play.

## Discord account

You must have one Discord account that meets Arena's current minimum account-age
requirement. Use that account as your Arena identity.

- **No alternate accounts.** Do not register or participate through another
  Discord account.
- **Keep your identity accurate.** Do not use another person's account or
  misrepresent who you are when registering or playing.

## Trust and player-safety history

To participate, you must not have a history that makes fair or safe play
unreasonable to expect. This includes:

- bans for cheating, hacking, or similar unfair-play activity; and
- recent severe toxic behaviour that significantly worsened other players'
  experience.

Staff may review relevant history and context when deciding eligibility. They may
refuse, suspend, or remove access where necessary to protect fair play and the
player experience.

## While participating

Eligibility is ongoing. Players must continue to meet these requirements and
follow [Participation and Conduct](/handbook/policies/participation-and-conduct/)
while using Arena.
